LumaMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

LumaMe is a personal screen-lighting app for iPhone. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Camera Access

LumaMe may request access to your camera for local preview features. Camera content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by LumaMe.

Microphone Access

LumaMe may request microphone access for sound-reactive lighting effects. Audio is used locally on your device for the feature and is not stored or uploaded by LumaMe.

Photo Library

If you choose to save captured photos or videos, LumaMe may request permission to add them to your Photos library.

In-App Purchases

LumaMe Pro is offered as a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. LumaMe does not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Collection

LumaMe does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal identification information.

Third-Party Services

Apple services such as the App Store and StoreKit may process information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

LumaMe does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LumaMe evolves. Any updates will be posted on this page.
