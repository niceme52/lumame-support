Personal screen lighting for iPhone.

About LumaMe

LumaMe turns your iPhone screen into a flexible personal light. It includes fill light, bedtime reading light, mood effects, custom colors, timers, and camera preview features.

Purchases

LumaMe Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. There is no recurring subscription.

Restore Purchases

Open LumaMe, go to Settings, and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Camera and Microphone

Camera access is used for preview features. Microphone access may be used for sound-reactive lighting effects.
